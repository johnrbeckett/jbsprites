# Overrides

Hand-corrected sprites. `scripts/make_sprites.py` copies each `<id>.webp` here over the cut sprite of the same name after cutting its sheet, so a re-cut keeps the fix. The editable source, `<id>-source.png`, is in the private `jbsprites-art` repo under `narnia/overrides/`. The sprite keeps its id, grid number and catalog tags.

| Sprite | Fix |
|---|---|
| 24-pevensies-at-work_02 | Feet were cropped and legs ran past the floor; redrawn with full legs and shoes, standing in front of the wardrobe, transparent background. |
